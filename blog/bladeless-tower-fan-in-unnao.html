Local conditions

Why a two-season fan makes sense in Unnao

Unnao sits between Kanpur and Lucknow on the Gangetic plain. Summers are long and hot, the weeks before the monsoon turn sticky, and winters bring fog and a real chill on early mornings.

A BLDC motor suits the long hours of a hot summer because BLDC motors are designed to draw less power than conventional induction motors. When the weather turns, the same fan can switch to warm wind mode on models that include it. Warm mode draws more power than cool mode, so use the timer and keep it to the rooms you are in.

Bladeless tower fan in Unnao: your questions answered

Is a bladeless tower fan good for Unnao?

Yes. A TheFutureX bladeless tower fan suits Unnao because one unit covers two seasons: a BLDC motor and strong speed for the long hot summer, and warm wind mode on models that include it for cold, foggy winter mornings. The remote lets you change settings from bed, and the blade-free loop is easy to live with around children.

Can one tower fan cover both summer and winter in Unnao?

Models with warm wind mode can blow cool air in summer and gentle warm air on cold mornings. Check that your model includes warm wind mode, since not every tower fan does.

Is warm wind mode safe to use overnight?

Use the timer so the fan switches itself off, keep it a safe distance from bedding and curtains, and never cover it. As with any heating appliance, do not leave it running unattended for long periods.

How do I run the fan economically through summer?

Use lower speeds when you can, set the timer at night and let sway spread the air. BLDC motors are designed to draw less power than conventional ones, but exact wattage depends on your model and setting.

Will it work in a large open room?

Tower fans suit bedrooms and small to medium rooms best. In a bigger room, put the fan near where people sit, and use sway and strong speed to reach more of the space.

Is the blade-free design safer around children?

There are no exposed spinning blades, which removes the main hazard of a conventional fan. Keep the fan on a flat, stable floor, keep the cable tidy and supervise young children.
