Quick answers

The questions buyers ask first

What is a bladeless tower fan?

A bladeless tower fan is a slim, upright fan that keeps its moving parts inside the base and releases air through an oval loop, so there are no exposed spinning blades. TheFutureX bladeless tower fans add a BLDC motor, a remote control and air care features to that design.

Is a bladeless tower fan better than a ceiling fan?

They do different jobs. A ceiling fan cools a whole room from above, while a bladeless tower fan can stand where you sit or sleep, move between rooms and be controlled by remote. For bedrooms, home offices and PG rooms a tower fan is often the easier fit, and many homes use both.

Is a bladeless fan safe for children and pets?

There are no exposed spinning blades, which removes the main hazard of a conventional fan. Keep the fan on a flat, stable floor, keep the cable tidy and supervise young children around any electrical appliance.

Does a BLDC motor really save electricity?

BLDC motors are generally designed to draw less power than conventional induction motors, which matters when a fan runs for many hours a day. The exact wattage depends on your model and speed setting, so check the label on your fan.

Is the TheFutureX tower fan quiet enough for sleeping?

TheFutureX tower fans are listed with silent operation, and the lower speeds are meant for night use. Set the timer so the fan switches itself off after you fall asleep.

What do the air quality lamp colours mean?

Red means the air quality is poor, yellow means average, blue means good and green means excellent.

Air care for polluted cities

Where air changes with traffic, season and nearby construction, a fan that shows you the air is more useful than one that only moves it.

The Free Press Journal reported in November 2025 that Pune's air quality index routinely sits between 101 and 200, with winter the worst season. Traffic corridors in Bengaluru, dust in Varanasi and winter haze across the Gangetic plain around Unnao make the same point from different directions: air quality shapes how a room feels.

The lamp tells you what the room looks like right now. Ion mode and the filter screens add air care to the airflow, and models with plasma or HEPA filtration, such as the TP12, go a step further. Keep windows closed on high-pollution days and check the filter screens often.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers first, with the details a buyer usually wants.

Does the fan purify the air?

It adds air care features to cooling: positive and negative oxygen ions, removable filter screens and, on models that include them, plasma and HEPA filtration. It is a comfort appliance and not a medical device, and results depend on the size and conditions of your room.

How do I remove and install the filter screen?

Switch the fan off and unplug it. To remove the screen, pull out the front handles outwards. To install it, align the filter screen with the groove and press it in.

Which features help in polluted cities like Pune?

Start with the air quality lamp, which shows red, yellow, blue or green so you can see when the room air needs attention. Ion mode and the removable filter screens add air care, and models with plasma and HEPA filtration, such as the TP12, go further. Keep windows closed on high-pollution days and check the filter screens often.

Can a bladeless tower fan heat a room in winter?

Models with warm wind mode can blow gentle warm air, which suits cooler mornings and evenings in places like Pune, Varanasi, Unnao and Falakata. It is made for personal comfort in a bedroom or small room, not for heating a large hall.

Does warm wind mode use more electricity?

Producing heat takes more power than moving air, so warm mode draws more than cool mode on any appliance. Use the timer, close the door and keep the fan a safe distance from curtains and bedding. Check your model's label for its heating wattage.

Does the fan have a warm mode for winter?

Models with warm wind mode can blow gentle warm air in the cooler months. When warm wind mode is on, the positive and negative oxygen ion function switches off automatically.

What does the Sway button do?

Sway spreads the airflow from side to side so more of the room receives air. Press Sway/Cancel on the remote to start or stop it.

How do I control the fan?

Use the remote for ON/OFF, speed up and down, sway, plasma and strong mode. The touch controls on the base cover ON/OFF, speed regulation, speed, timing, ion and disinfection.

What size room suits a tower fan?

Tower fans suit bedrooms, home offices and small to medium living rooms. In a large hall, place the fan near where people sit instead of in a far corner, and use sway to spread the airflow.

What warranty does a TheFutureX tower fan come with?

Product listings show a 12 months warranty on the TP09 series and a 1 year warranty on the TP12. Keep your invoice and check the product page for your model's current terms.

Can I leave the fan running all night?

Use the timer so the fan turns itself off after you fall asleep. That is good practice with any electrical appliance, and it keeps the room cool through the part of the night when you need it most.

How do I clean the fan?

Switch off and unplug the fan, wipe the body and the loop with a soft dry cloth, and remove the filter screen with the steps in the filter question above.
