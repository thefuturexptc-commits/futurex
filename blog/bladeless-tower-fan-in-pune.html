Why air quality changes the fan decision in Pune

A November 2025 Free Press Journal report says Pune's air quality index routinely runs between 101 and 200, the moderate to unhealthy range, and that winter is the worst season. It points to a fleet that adds nearly 2 lakh vehicles a year, with two-wheelers making up 73 percent of motorised vehicles.

That changes what a fan should do. In Hinjewadi, Wakad and Baner towers, windows face construction and traffic, and families decide each morning whether to open them. A fan with an air quality lamp turns that decision into a colour. Ion mode and the filter screens add air care, and the TP12's plasma and HEPA filtration go further.

Red means the fan reads the air quality as poor. Closing windows on those days and checking the filter screens is a sensible response. If you have a health condition, follow your doctor's advice and not a fan's lamp.
