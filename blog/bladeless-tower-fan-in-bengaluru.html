Local conditions

Why Bengaluru homes are changing how they cool

IMD readings reported by Deccan Herald show the city's highest April temperature rising from 34.6°C in 2011 to 36.7°C in 2022, with an all-time April high of 39.2°C recorded in 2016. IISc's T V Ramachandra, quoted in the same report, links the trend to lost green cover and spreading concrete.

Much of Bengaluru lives in compact spaces: gated apartments in Electronic City and Whitefield, PG rooms near tech parks, small bedrooms in HSR Layout and Koramangala. In those rooms a ceiling fan is fixed and a pedestal fan eats floor space. A tower fan stands in a corner, moves to the desk in the day and to the bedside at night.

Bladeless tower fan in Bengaluru: your questions answered

Is a bladeless tower fan good for Bengaluru?

Yes. A TheFutureX bladeless tower fan suits Bengaluru flats, PG rooms and home offices because it runs quietly, stands on a slim base, takes remote control from the bed or desk, and shows room air quality with a four-colour lamp. Pick the TP09 series for everyday cooling or the TP12 for added air care.

Which bladeless tower fan suits a PG room in Electronic City?

A single slim unit with a remote and a timer. The TP09 series covers everyday cooling. Choose the TP12 if you also want HEPA filtration and plasma purification. Tower fans suit bedrooms and small rooms, which is what most PG rooms are.

Is a bladeless tower fan quiet enough for work calls?

TheFutureX tower fans are listed with silent operation, and the lower speeds are meant for quiet use. Place the fan slightly to the side of your microphone and use sway so the air does not blow straight at you.

Do I need warm wind mode in Bengaluru?

Bengaluru winters are mild, so cool mode does most of the work through the year. Warm wind mode, on models that include it, is a bonus for cooler mornings and not a necessity.

How do I keep a tower fan clean in a dusty Bengaluru flat?

Switch it off and unplug it, wipe the body and loop with a soft dry cloth, and take the filter screen out by pulling the front handles outwards. The air quality lamp tells you when the room air needs attention.

Will a tower fan fit a small Bengaluru bedroom?

Yes. It stands on a slim round base and does not need wall or ceiling fixing, so it fits beside a bed or desk and can be moved to another room when you need it.
