Will a fan help with mosquitoes in the Dooars?

Moving air can make it harder for insects to settle near you, but a fan does not replace nets or repellents. Use it alongside the protection you already use.

Is a bladeless fan a good choice for homes with small children?

There are no exposed spinning blades, which removes the main hazard of a conventional fan. Stand it on a flat, stable floor, keep the cable tidy and supervise young children.

Do winter evenings in Falakata need a warm mode?

Evenings turn cool quickly after sunset. Warm wind mode, on models that include it, gives gentle warmth in a bedroom or family room. Check that your model includes it before you buy.

How do I look after the fan during heavy rain?

Keep it on a dry, flat floor away from damp walls and splashes, wipe the body with a soft dry cloth, and unplug it before cleaning.

Can it serve a shop-front room?

Yes for a small room, with the same care as any appliance. Keep the fan clear of stock and curtains, and use the timer so it does not run unattended for long.
