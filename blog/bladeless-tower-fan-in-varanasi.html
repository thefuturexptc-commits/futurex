Local conditions

Why still air is the problem in Varanasi homes

Varanasi sits on the Gangetic plain, where summers are long and hot and winters bring fog and haze. Many homes around Lanka, Bhelupur and the old city are built close together with thick walls and small windows, so air can sit still for hours even when the day is hot.

A tower fan keeps that air moving without taking over the floor. Sway spreads airflow across a living room, strong speed helps through peak heat, and the filter screens come out by hand when dust builds up. In winter, warm wind mode on models that include it gives a comfortable bedroom without a separate heater.

Bladeless tower fan in Varanasi: your questions answered

Is a bladeless tower fan good for Varanasi?

The slim base fits small rooms in Lanka, Sigra and the old city.

Is a tower fan better than a pedestal fan for narrow-lane homes?

A tower fan has a slim round base and no wide stand, so it fits small rooms more easily. It is easy to move between rooms, and the loop has no exposed spinning blades to catch cloth or fingers.

How do I handle dust in a Varanasi home?

Wipe the body and loop with a soft dry cloth after switching off and unplugging the fan. Take the filter screen out by pulling the front handles outwards, and press it back into the groove when done.

Will it cool a joint-family living room?

Tower fans suit small to medium rooms. In a larger room, place the fan near where people sit, use sway to spread the air, and use strong speed for peak heat.

Do I need a separate heater for Varanasi winters?

Warm wind mode on models that include it gives gentle warmth in a bedroom or small room. It is for personal comfort and not for heating a large hall. Check that your model includes it.

Can I use it in a shop with a room above?

Yes for the room, with the same care as any appliance. Keep the fan on a flat, stable floor, away from curtains and stored goods, and use the timer so it does not run unattended for long.
